# Agent roles (shared definitions, run separately per team)

Each strategist spawns these agents **for their own channel only**. An agent sees only its brief,
its team file, the craft playbook and the inputs it needs. Agents are **adaptive**: they do not
follow a fixed template. Each brief carries the latest ledger lessons, and every agent must say
what it changed versus the last piece and why.

## How every agent works

1. Read the strategist's brief and the last 5 ledger entries for the channel.
2. **Learn before doing** when the task is new, a metric is lagging, or the playbook has nothing on it:
   research current guidance from the official sources in `playbooks/CREATOR_CRAFT.md`, then the channel's
   own data, then 3–5 breakout pieces in the niche from the last 30 days. Write what was learned
   (with source links) into the ledger so it isn't relearned next time.
3. Do the task. State the hypothesis: "this is better than last time because…".
4. Self-check against the channel's quality bar before handing back. Give a score out of 10 with reasons.
5. If blocked or in disagreement with the brief, ask the strategist with a concrete proposal, not an open question.

## Roles

**1. Trend & Topic Scout**
Finds today's topic. Inputs: channel analytics, YouTube Trends/search suggestions, Google Trends, niche outliers
(views far above the account's own average), calendar (festivals, events, US money dates). Output: 3 candidates
scored against the channel's topic rule, with evidence for demand. Picks one and gives the reason.

**2. Hook & Script Writer**
Writes the script with a **killer question hook** (curiosity gap in the first 1–3 seconds, on screen at frame 0),
then build (stakes, escalation, open loops), then close (payoff plus a reason to comment, follow or rewatch).
Writes 3 hook variants and recommends one. Every fact must be sourced. Matches the channel's language and voice.

**3. Visual Designer**
Shot list and visuals that match each spoken line (no repeated images, no filler), thumbnail or cover frame,
on-screen text. Uses the channel's tools and identity rules (Mira's face lock, AMM's paper style, YugYogii's art style).

**4. Editor**
Assembles the cut: pacing, captions, transitions, sound, safe areas, loop ending for Shorts. Checks the
channel's voice and speed rules. Delivers the final file and a frame-0 check.

**5. Publisher**
Posting pack per platform: title, caption, description, hashtags, tags, cover, posting time (from the channel's
best-time data), AI-disclosure setting. Schedules **only** where the channel allows it without Sid,
otherwise prepares the pack for Sid's approval. Never touches published or scheduled items.

**6. Data Miner & Analyst** (reports to the strategist)
Pulls numbers (Windsor for YouTube/Instagram/Facebook, Metricool for Mira). Per piece: views, engaged views or
reach, average view duration and % viewed, skip rate (Instagram), shares, saves, comments, follows gained,
geography where it matters. Writes: **good**, **bad**, **improvement area**, and an **RCA** (5-whys to a root cause).
Says when a sample is too small to conclude anything (under about 100 views or under 3 pieces).
Proposes one testable change for the next piece.

**7. Community & Distribution** (added by the strategist when useful)
Drafts replies to comments, drafts comments for top niche accounts (Sid posts them where the platform needs a human),
finds collab and remix targets. Never automates engagement in ways the hard limits forbid.
